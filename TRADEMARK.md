# Trademark policy

The code in this repository is open source under MIT OR Apache-2.0. Those
licences give you the code. They do not give you the name or the logo. Apache
2.0 says so in its section 6. MIT says nothing about trademarks, so this file
states it for both.

"Onde", "Onde Inference", "Onde Desktop" and the Onde logo are trademarks of
Seto Elkahfi (Onde Inference). The apps are distributed by Splitfire AB.

## What you can do

- Fork the code, change it, and ship it, under the licence.
- Say your project is built with Onde, powered by Onde, or compatible with
  Onde CLI 0.x.
- Link to the website or this repository.
- Write about Onde in articles, reviews and tutorials, and use the name to do
  so.

## What you can't do

- Use "Onde", "Onde Inference" or "Onde Desktop" as the name of a fork, a
  competing product, an SDK, or an app or listing in any store.
- Use the Onde logo or the app icon for anything that is not the official
  project.
- Say or suggest that your build is official, endorsed by us, or affiliated
  with us.
- Use the name or logo in a way that could make someone think your product
  comes from us.

## If you fork

Pick your own name and your own icon before you publish a build. The Onde
artwork is in `assets/` and `packaging/app/src-tauri/icons/`. Replace both. A
line such as "based on Onde CLI" in the README is fine and appreciated.

Store listings count. A copy of the app published under our name or icon is a
trademark problem even though the code is free to copy.

## Questions

Open an issue in this repository, or ask before you publish if you are unsure.
